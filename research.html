University of California, Berkeley

Hybrid Quantum–Classical Image Denoising

Research collaboration and benchmark design

Advised by Phillip Kerger.

Hybrid quantum–classical image denoising and benchmark design for controlled comparisons of quantum-layer contributions, reconstruction quality, and computational cost.

Benchmark design
  • Matched classical and quantum modules within a shared image-reconstruction architecture.
  • Frozen-parameter and no-entanglement ablations to isolate the quantum layer’s contribution.
  • Comparisons across training-data sizes and image-noise levels.
  • Joint assessment of reconstruction quality, parameter count, and training and inference time.
