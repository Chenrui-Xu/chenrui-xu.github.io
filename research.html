Journal of Computing in Civil Engineering (JCCE)

Towards a Framework for Comparing Operating Energy Use and Estimated Energy Savings: A Case Study of AI-Assisted Building Fault Detection and Diagnostics

Chenrui Xu, Burcu Akinci, Christopher McComb

Invited manuscriptIn preparation

Using a building fault detection and diagnostics case study, this work develops a framework for comparing the energy required to operate a digital platform with its estimated task-level energy savings over the same analysis horizon.

ASCE International Conference on Computing in Civil Engineering · i3CE 2026

Toward a Time-Aware Assessment Framework for the Carbon Cost of AI-Enabled Decarbonization

Chenrui Xu, Burcu Akinci, Christopher McComb

AcceptedarXiv preprint · 2026

This paper represents AI-induced and avoided emissions as time-varying streams over a finite horizon. It examines how timing and time preference affect the assessment of AI-enabled decarbonization, including carbon payback and comparisons between interventions.
