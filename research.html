UC Berkeley · MEng team capstone · 2024–2025

Benchmarking Transformer Models for Financial Time-Series Forecasting

As part of UC Berkeley’s MEng fintech capstone, I worked on Transformer-based financial time-series forecasting. I later developed a public-data benchmark comparing an LSTM baseline with compact implementations of iTransformer, PatchTST, Informer, and FEDformer across six U.S. equities and ETFs. The PyTorch workflow combines data preparation, chronological evaluation, configuration-based training, and saved experiments.

My contribution: Capstone team member; developer of the individual public-data extension (2026).

University of Edinburgh · Undergraduate team research · 2024

How Universities Introduce Mathematical Proof: Curriculum Mapping and Student Perspectives

I contributed to a fourth-year team study of introductory proof courses at UK universities. We compared Russell Group curricula, developed a content classification framework, and analysed student surveys about confidence, understanding, and the role of proof. The report combines syllabus analysis, descriptive statistics, and coding of written responses to examine students’ transition to university mathematics.

My contribution: Coauthor of the fourth-year team project.

UC Berkeley · IEOR C253 · 2025

Pricing and Matching Policies for Shared Mobility

In a Berkeley team project, I studied pricing and rider matching for shared mobility. I later extended the work with an event-driven simulator, a fitted conversion-response model, and an approximate dynamic programming pricing policy. The implementation explores interactions among price, queue state, shared-ride costs, and waiting time.

My contribution: Waymodidi team member; developer of the individual simulator and pricing extension (2026).

UC Berkeley · Team course project · 2024

Optimizing Airbnb Prices and Property Selection

In a six-person course project, I explored how demand modeling and optimization can guide short-term rental pricing and property selection. Our team used New York Airbnb listings to combine an approximate multinomial logit choice model with mixed-integer optimization in Python and Gurobi, examining the assumptions behind proxy demand and choice probabilities.

My contribution: Member of the six-person project team.

University of Edinburgh · Team course project · 2024

Predicting Hotel Booking Cancellations with Statistical Learning

Working with Yu Zhang, I investigated hotel booking cancellations using more than 119,000 reservation records. Our analysis combined data cleaning, exploratory visualization, logistic regression, regularization, and random forests. We compared classification metrics and examined informative booking attributes, connecting interpretable statistical modeling with predictive machine learning.

My contribution: Coauthor with Yu Zhang.

University of Edinburgh · Individual course project · 2023

Modeling Seasonal and Spatial Patterns in Scottish Weather

I used R to investigate seasonal precipitation patterns across Scottish weather stations. The analysis combines permutation tests, harmonic regression, and geographic predictors including latitude, longitude, and elevation. I evaluated predictions at stations excluded from model fitting and compared errors across locations and months.

My contribution: Individual statistical analysis and report.

University of Edinburgh · Individual coursework · 2022–2023

Computational Modelling of Nonlinear Dynamics and Epidemic Systems

I used Python to investigate nonlinear differential equations through symbolic stability analysis, numerical integration, and phase portraits. The coursework includes nonlinear oscillators, the Rössler system, and course-specified epidemic scenarios involving reduced transmission, waning immunity, and vaccination. The reports connect mathematical structure, parameter experiments, and visual interpretation.

My contribution: Individual implementation and analysis of course-specified models.
